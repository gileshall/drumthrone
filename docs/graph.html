Similarity graph

Each dot is a solo, gray for the 20 recorded solos and magenta for the 32 generated ones, sized by overall score (key next to the buttons). Lines connect each solo to the three it's most similar to, across all the scoring measurements.

Springs: a force-directed layout of the similarity network; dots can be dragged. PCA: the two directions along which the recorded solos differ most. UMAP: a map that keeps each solo close to its nearest neighbors. With hover sound on (top right), hovering a dot plays a few seconds of that solo; the recordings are linked from their own pages. Click a dot to open its page.
