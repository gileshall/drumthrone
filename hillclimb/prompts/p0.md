Using Python 3 and mido (no other MIDI libraries), write a single script, drum_solo.py, that generates the best two minute drum solo you can as a General MIDI file, solo.mid, on MIDI channel 10. It will be played back on a General MIDI synthesizer.

Make it sound like a great drummer playing:
- Develop motifs: state them, vary them, bring them back, and contrast density and dynamics to build tension and release.
- Give it a consistent feel: deliberate placement (swing, pushing ahead of or laying back behind the beat) and ghost notes against accents, rather than random jitter.
- Keep it playable by one drummer: at most two hands and two feet striking at any instant.
- The whole General MIDI percussion set (notes 35–81) is yours, not just the drum kit; use what serves the music.

Running the script in an empty directory must write solo.mid there, identical on every run. Share drum_solo.py.
